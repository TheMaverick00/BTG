Core Service Pillar 01

Engineering Services & Technical Consulting

Process & Facility Engineering

Concept design, feasibility studies, debottlenecking, and process optimization for refineries, petrochemical plants, LNG/CNG terminals, and power facilities.

Project Management & EPC Advisory
